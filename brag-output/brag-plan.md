# Brag Plan: Hatua (Trust First. Then Act.)

## What is this app?
Hatua is an operational, trust-gated climate action layer on Kenya's Conduit@Empathy climate station at JKUAT that detects hardware failures before dispatching life-saving actions over USSD and SMS.

## The angle
Most weather apps show pretty charts while ignoring that the physical station's rain gauge is literally dead and gust direction is corrupted. Hatua catches the hardware lies first, then routes grounded operational actions to campus crews and smallholder tomato growers via Africa's Talking USSD and TypeSafe Jev AI.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A stark, high-contrast warning banner: "0.4 mm of rain in 5 days. Rain Gauge 2 is dead."
Punchline: "Stop looking at the sky. Trust first. Then act."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Hardware Autopsy (3s-7s):** Visualizing Conduit Station 61's dual rain gauge failure (Gauge 2 stuck at 0.0) and nightly health code corruption.
- **The Trust-Gated Action Engine (7s-12s):** Automated heat stroke protocols for JKUAT groundsmen and tomato greenhouse ventilation triggers.
- **Last-Mile Africa's Talking & Jev (12s-15s):** Dialing `*384*61#` on feature phones with TypeSafe Jev AI Swahili/English routing.

## Outro / punchline (15s-18s)
"Hatua: Operational Climate Intelligence for Kenya. Built for Hack The Weather 2026."

## User flow worth showing
1. Ingest raw Conduit@Empathy telemetry → 2. Trust gate flags hardware failure → 3. Real-time USSD/SMS dispatch to Estates Lead & Juja farmers.
